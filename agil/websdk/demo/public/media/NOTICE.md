# Third-party media — demo only

The interactive demo call on the landing page uses a short excerpt of
**Big Buck Bunny** as the video feed of its synthetic participants. It is a
demo/fixture asset and is **not** used for real users or in the widget bundle.

The Wiki also shows frames from this clip in screenshots of the visual editor
and the camera settings. The capture browser plays the excerpt as a simulated
camera. The screenshots show actual application controls.

| File | What it is | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `bbb-360p-10s.mp4` | 10.0 s excerpt (film 02:12–02:22), H.264 High, 640×360, 30 fps, no audio | ~0.44 MB |
| `bbb-360p-10s.jpg` | poster frame extracted from the clip (640×360) | ~27 KB |
